## Storyboard 1

Bob, a university student in a group project that is running on low budget. He wants the AI to give him advice on the direction of tackling the project with this constraint constantly in mind, even after many message exchanges. He marks this as important so the system prioritises this when selecting context for future responses. Now as the conversation grows, the context space becomes limited, but Bob will know that this constraint will still remain priotised, and in more recent messages, the AI will still recommend free tools and will not forget this constraint.
(the sitution under scenario 1)

## Storyboard 2

Bob, the same university student in the group project have now completed the development stage and no longer needs to have the constraint marked as important, because now he will work on creating the presentation slides for demostration. In order to keep the limited space efficiently used, he now needs to go back and remove the initial constraint, no longer having it marked as important. Now the AI will no longer prioritise this constraint for future responses when the context space is limited, and will only focus on using more recent messages and flagged messages.
(the sitution under scenario 2)

## Storyboard 3

Bob, the same university student working on the group project, marks another message as important. However, the combined size of his important messages now exceeds the available budget. The system shows a warning and pauses response generation until he resolves this conflict. Bob reviews his flagged messages and removes some flagged information that is no longer needed. The system recalculates the context, and he then continues the conversation.
(the sitution under scenario 3)
