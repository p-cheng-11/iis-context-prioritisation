## Storyboard 1

Bob, a university student in a group project that is running on low budget. He wants the AI to give him advice on the direction of tackling the project with this constraint constantly in mind, even after many message exchanges. He marks this as important so the system prioritises this when selecting context for future responses. Now as the conversation grows, the context space becomes limited, but Bob will know that this constraint will still remain priotised, and in more recent messages, the AI will still recommend free tools and will not forget this constraint.
